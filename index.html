2 · Parameters adjust and recompute

Everything recomputes instantly. Spread and commission are charged on every simulated trade.

All result cards (rolling windows, Monte Carlo, equity, edge) use the in-sample period only: from "In-sample from" to the start of the out-of-sample. The out-of-sample is sealed for the walk-forward. Outcomes are evaluated against the account limits set below; the optional risk guard cuts earlier, with a safety margin.

Does it survive the limits? · rolling windows main result

Simulates a fresh evaluation (account reset, same limits) starting on many different dates and counts how many pass (reach the target), how many breach a limit (daily loss of 5% of the initial balance or total drawdown of 10%) and how many run out of time without reaching the target. The minimum number of trading days is also required.

Each simulated evaluation, by start date
Risk-per-trade sweep · same strategy, same windows

Sweeps "Risk per trade" from 0.25% to 3% and shows the pass / breach / no-target split for each value: a direct way to choose how much to risk under the limits.

Breach risk · Monte Carlo drawdown distribution

Resamples the order of your trades thousands of times (bootstrap) so the result does not depend on a single historical sequence. Gives the probability of passing and, above all, the drawdown distribution: the real risk of breaching the account.

Distribution of the final result

Equity curve · full in-sample (context, not the verdict)
Statistical evidence Does the strategy have its own edge, or does it just ride the market? This does not decide whether it survives the limits, but it tells you how much to trust that it will repeat.

Statistical edge is there a real edge?

Measures whether the strategy wins by edge or by luck, independently of the account limits. Expectancy is the average gain per trade in multiples of the risk ("R"). The t-stat tells whether that edge is statistically reliable.

Selection vs chance · permutation test does picking the strongest add anything?

In a rising market any long-only strategy wins through beta. Here the rotation is compared with 2,000 rotations that pick an asset at random on the same days the rotation is invested, with the same costs. If the rotation does not beat 95% of the random ones (p < 0.05), there is no selection skill: the result is beta, not edge.

Walk-forward · out-of-sample the anti-self-deception test

The panel optimises only the strategy's own parameters on the in-sample period and applies them, unchanged, to the period it never saw (out-of-sample). With the preloaded data the out-of-sample starts on 1 January 2024 (sealed holdout); the date is editable. It measures what matters: whether, out of sample, the strategy passes or breaches.
